Here are the notes from three user interviews about our clinic scheduling tool. Synthesize them.

--- P1 (receptionist, 2026-09-02) ---
"Mornings are chaos. I have the phone in one hand and the paper book in the other. If two people call for the same slot I only find out when both show up."
"The doctors change their hours by texting me. I copy it into the book, sometimes a day late."
"I don't trust the online thing we tried, it double-booked twice in a week."

--- P2 (clinic manager, 2026-09-03) ---
"Double bookings are the number one complaint I get, from patients and from doctors."
"Every doctor keeps their own calendar. I ask them for their hours by email on Friday and half of them answer on Tuesday."
"I want to see the whole week for the whole clinic on one screen. Right now I can't."

--- P3 (physiotherapist, 2026-09-04) ---
"I set my own hours and I change them a lot. Whatever tool we use has to let me change them myself, not through the front desk."
"Double booking happened to me once; I had two patients in the corridor. Never again."
"Reminders would be nice, patients forget Monday appointments."
