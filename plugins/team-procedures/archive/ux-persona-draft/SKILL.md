## When to use

Do not. This procedure is retired. Use `ux-interview-synthesis` and, if a persona is still
needed, write it from the themes in the synthesis file, citing the theme ids.

## Steps

1. (retired) Read the interview notes.
2. (retired) Pick a representative participant and generalize.
3. (retired) Write name, goals, frustrations, quote.

## Decisions behind this

- Retired because step 2 is the problem: "representative" was chosen by the author, so the
  persona said what the author already believed. The synthesis counts participants instead.

## Gotchas

- A persona with a stock photo and a name gets quoted in meetings as if it were a person
  who was interviewed. It was not. If you write one, put "composite of <n> participants"
  under the name.
- Personas outlive the research they came from. Date them like every other artifact.
